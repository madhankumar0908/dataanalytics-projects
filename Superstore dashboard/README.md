# Superstore Sales Dashboard & Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Sample Superstore** dataset to uncover
sales, profit, order, customer, product, and regional performance
insights.

The analysis is built in **Microsoft Excel** and includes calculated
metrics, pivot-table analysis, and a sales dashboard designed to make
business trends easier to understand.

## 📁 Project Contents

-   `Superstore.csv (2).xlsx` --- Main Excel workbook containing the
    dataset, metrics, pivot tables, and dashboard.
-   **Sample - Superstore** --- Raw Superstore transaction data.
-   **metrics** --- Key business metrics such as Total Sales, Total
    Profit, and Total Orders.
-   **pivot table** --- Pivot-table analysis covering regional, segment,
    and monthly sales.
-   **Dashboard** --- Sales dashboard layout for presenting the analysis
    visually.

## 🔎 Dataset

The dataset contains **9,994 transaction records** and includes
information about:

-   Orders and shipping dates
-   Customers and customer segments
-   Geographic regions, states, and cities
-   Product categories and sub-categories
-   Sales, quantity, discounts, and profit
-   Shipping modes

### Key Columns

  Column          Description
  --------------- ------------------------------------
  Order ID        Unique order identifier
  Order Date      Date the order was placed
  Ship Date       Date the order was shipped
  Ship Mode       Shipping method used
  Customer Name   Customer associated with the order
  Segment         Customer segment
  Region          Sales region
  Category        Product category
  Sub-Category    Product sub-category
  Sales           Revenue generated
  Quantity        Number of products sold
  Discount        Discount applied
  Profit          Profit generated

## 📈 Key Metrics

The workbook calculates the following headline metrics:

-   **Total Sales:** 2,297,200.86
-   **Total Profit:** 286,397.02
-   **Total Orders:** 9,995

These metrics provide a high-level view of overall business performance
before drilling into regional, segment, and time-based trends.

## 📌 Analysis Areas

### Regional Performance

Sales are analyzed across the major regions to identify the strongest
and weakest geographic markets.

### Customer Segment Analysis

The project compares sales across customer segments to understand which
customer groups contribute most to revenue.

### Monthly Sales Trends

Monthly sales analysis helps identify seasonality, growth patterns, and
periods of higher or lower demand.

### Product Performance

Category and sub-category information can be used to evaluate
product-level sales and profitability.

### Profitability

Sales are considered alongside discounts and profit to highlight the
difference between revenue generation and actual business profitability.
