# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI data visualization project** designed to analyze employee data and provide insights into workforce composition, employee attrition, job satisfaction, salary distribution, experience, age, gender, and departmental performance.

The dashboard helps HR teams and management understand employee trends and identify areas that may require attention, supporting **data-driven HR decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze the overall employee workforce.
- Monitor employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Understand employee distribution across departments.
- Analyze attrition based on salary slabs.
- Study employee age groups and workforce distribution.
- Analyze attrition by gender.
- Understand the relationship between experience and attrition.
- Analyze job roles and job satisfaction.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making.

The dashboard provides important HR metrics such as:

- 📈 **Attrition Rate %**
- 🎂 **Average Age**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains multiple interactive visualizations for detailed HR analysis.

### 1. Attrition by Department

A **Donut Chart** is used to analyze employee attrition across different departments.

**Purpose:**
- Identify departments with higher attrition.
- Compare attrition levels between departments.
- Help HR teams focus retention strategies.

---

### 2. Employee Attrition by Salary Slab

A **Clustered Bar Chart** analyzes employee counts by salary slab and attrition status.

**Purpose:**
- Understand how salary levels relate to attrition.
- Compare employees who stayed with those who left.
- Identify salary ranges that may have higher employee turnover.

---

### 3. Job Role & Job Satisfaction

A **Matrix/Pivot Table** analyzes:

- Job Role
- Job Satisfaction
- Attrition Count

**Purpose:**
- Identify job roles with higher attrition.
- Understand employee satisfaction across different roles.
- Support employee engagement and retention initiatives.

---

### 4. Employee Distribution by Age Group

A **Column Chart** shows employee distribution across different age groups.

**Purpose:**
- Understand workforce demographics.
- Identify dominant employee age groups.
- Support workforce planning.

---

### 5. Attrition by Gender

A **Pie Chart** analyzes employee attrition based on gender.

**Purpose:**
- Compare attrition levels across genders.
- Identify workforce retention patterns.

---

### 6. Attrition by Total Experience

An **Area Chart** analyzes attrition based on total professional experience.

**Purpose:**
- Understand how employee experience relates to attrition.
- Identify experience levels with increased employee turnover.
- Support career development and retention planning.

---

### 7. Employees by Department

A **Funnel Chart** provides a department-level view of employee distribution.

**Purpose:**
- Compare workforce size across departments.
- Understand departmental workforce composition.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to dynamically filter the analysis.

### Department Filter

Users can select a specific department and analyze its employee and attrition information.

### Age Group Filter

Users can filter the dashboard based on employee age groups.

These filters make the dashboard interactive and allow users to perform focused HR analysis.
